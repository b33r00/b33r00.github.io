Roadmap

blog thoughts 2026

This is not a prescription for anyone else. It is a record of where I am going and why, written down so I can hold myself to it.

current focus

Web application security and Active Directory. These two areas cover the majority of real-world attack surface in corporate environments, and I want to be solid at both before spreading further. The goal is not to collect techniques but to understand the underlying trust model well enough to find paths that are not in the checklists.

short term

  • Finish HTB CPTS and CWES paths
  • Consistent CTF participation — at least one per month
  • First public bug bounty finding
  • Document methodology properly, not just collect notes

medium term

  • OSCP — when the methodology is solid enough that the exam is confirmation, not a goal
  • Deeper Active Directory lab — multi-domain, trust relationships, modern defenses
  • Build something useful — a tool, a scanner, something that reflects how I think about a problem

longer term

  • CVE research — find something real, go through the disclosure process
  • Red team ops, not just individual technique practice
  • Contribute back — writeups, tools, or something that helps someone else start

what I am not doing

Chasing certifications for their own sake. A cert is useful if the preparation teaches something I did not already know. Otherwise it is just a badge. I am also not trying to cover every domain — hardware, blockchain, mobile, forensics all exist and I touch them in CTFs, but they are not the focus right now.

how this gets updated

When something changes significantly — a goal completed, a direction shifted, something learned that changes what I think matters — I will update this. It is not a static document.
